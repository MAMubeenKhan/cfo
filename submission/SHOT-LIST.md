* **Recorder:** press **Windows + Alt + R** (Xbox Game Bar) while the browser is in front, or use OBS. Record at 1920×1080. Windows saves to `Videos\Captures`.
* **Browser:** one window, full screen, zoom 100%, light mode, no bookmarks bar, close every other tab, silence notifications (Windows + N, or turn on Do Not Disturb).
* **Have these open in separate tabs, in this order:**
  1. https://cryptid-field-office-mubeen9.vercel.app
  2. The Case Board (Sanity Dashboard sidebar, or https://www.sanity.io/@oe22r9q65/application/v6xuv6jzd4yofy0qrrasddn7)
  3. https://cryptid-field-office.sanity.studio/ (open a case that is in "Director's review")
* **Have this text ready to paste** (Notepad is fine):
  * Title: `Silent light above the salt flats`
  * Account: `I was parked at a pull-off around 10pm when a single white light rose slowly from behind the ridge, hovered for about two minutes, then accelerated horizontally out of sight in under a second. There was no sound and no trail. A second driver stopped and saw the same thing.`
  * Place to search: `Wendover, Utah`
* **Photo to attach:** `seed\images\c10-capsule.png` in the project folder.
* Afterwards, tell me the case number you filed (for example CFO-2026-0019) and I will hide it so it does not clutter the public site.

* Do not show any password, token or the Vercel or Sanity settings pages.
