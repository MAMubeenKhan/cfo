## How to record a clip (Windows)
1. Open the page you need in your browser, full screen.
2. Press **Windows + Alt + R** to start. A small bar appears. Press the same keys to stop.
3. The file is saved in `C:\Users\Mubeen\Videos\Captures`.
4. Move the mouse **slowly**, and pause about 2 seconds after each thing you do.
5. Copy the files into `C:\Users\Mubeen\sanity\submission\clips\` and name them exactly `caseboard.mp4` and `studio.mp4`.
6. Do not show any settings pages, tokens or passwords.

If you make a mistake, stop and record again. Short clips are easy to redo.
